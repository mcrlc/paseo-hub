## Component use in the card (src/daemons/sprites/panel.tsx on the PR head)
Card (title, description) > FailureAlert (remove) | EmptyState or RecordList > RecordRow(actions=RowActions > ConfirmMenuItem) > TwoLine;
form grid gap-4 > NoticeAlert (success) | FailureAlert > FormField kind=text (Name) > FormField kind=secret (Value) > FormActions > Button.
No className on shared components, no margins, no weights, no tracking/uppercase; src/typography-policy.test.ts passes (6-gates.txt).
The panel renders the token Card and the environment Card as adjacent siblings in a fragment (panel.tsx SpritesSettingsPanel);
Card (src/components/app/card.tsx) owns no outer margin, so nothing spaces them: F1.
